01 / FEATURES
A digital twin workbench, not a file viewer.

      
Everything lives in one scene with real coordinates, terrain and time of day — so a clash with the ground, a shadow on a neighbour or a sensor on a girder is exactly where it is.

    

    

      

        
stream3D Tiles

        
        
Massive BIM, streamed

        
IFC and Revit as OGC 3D Tiles with all property sets on board. Filter by IfcClass or Revit category, click any element for its properties.

      

      

        
cutIfcAlignment

        
        
Sections by station

        
Cut a bridge or tunnel perpendicular to its IFC 4x3 alignment at km 0+192.40 — with a live 2D cross section that follows the slider.

      

      

        
placeEPSG

        
        
Georeferencing that holds

        
IfcMapConversion, national grids and geoid models (GCG2016, EGM2008) — or drop a model on the map and nudge it with a gizmo.

      

      

        
simulateWEA

        
        
Wind turbine shadows

        
Place parametric turbines on real terrain and run a daily shadow-flicker analysis against real buildings.

      

      

        
monitorSensorThings

        
        
Live sensors on the model

        
OGC SensorThings (FROST, MQTT) bridge monitoring with sparklines and damage events, plus live gauge levels.

      

      

        
build4D

        
        
Construction sequencing

        
Play construction stages straight from IFC properties on a timeline — and share the sequence as a clean StageTwin link.

      

      

        
capturereality

        
        
Point clouds & splats

        
Scans with Eye Dome Lighting and colour by height or class; photoreal Gaussian splats as native 3D Tiles.

      

      

        
reviewBCF

        
        
Annotate & inspect

        
Point and area annotations with category and priority, an inspection workflow for bridges, and BCF 2.1 export.

      

      

        
explorewalk

        
        
Walk through it

        
First person with WASD or gamepad and wall collision, or third person with a character — on terrain, in the model, underground.

03 / UNDER THE HOOD
IFC in, 3D Tiles out — in seconds.

      
Uploaded IFC files run through our own tiler: geometry on the ifc-lite Rust kernel, then compressed OGC 3D Tiles with every property set — ready to stream.

    

    

      
[1] parse
IFC 2x3 · 4 · 4x3
Alignments, linear placements, sectioned solids — the infrastructure bits other kernels trip over.

      
[2] place
World coordinates
Map conversion, EPSG by code or name, geoid-corrected heights, footprint-centred anchors.

      
[3] tile
glTF + meshopt
Quantised, meshopt-compressed tiles with EXT_structural_metadata.

      
[4] stream
CesiumJS
Level-of-detail streaming on the globe, with filters, sections and sensors on top.
